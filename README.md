# Retail Financial & Audit Risk Analytics

## Project Summary

This project analyzes the financial performance and potential audit-risk indicators of Walmart, Target, and Costco using five years of publicly available financial-statement data.

The analysis will use Python, pandas, Excel, and Tableau to compare profitability, liquidity, inventory efficiency, cash-flow quality, and financial reporting risks across the three companies.

## Key Questions

- Which company has demonstrated the strongest revenue and profitability growth?
- How efficiently does each company manage inventory?
- Does operating cash flow consistently support reported net income?
- Which financial trends may indicate elevated audit or business risk?
- How do the companies compare in liquidity and leverage?

## Planned Metrics

- Revenue growth
- Gross margin
- Operating margin
- Inventory turnover
- Days inventory outstanding
- Operating cash flow to net income
- Current ratio
- Debt-to-equity ratio
